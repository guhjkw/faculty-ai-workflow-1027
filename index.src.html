How it works

Four steps. One is yours, and nothing reaches "final" without it.

01IngestPublisher PowerPoints, syllabi, readings, your decks and notes go into a private knowledge base, split by slide and section, each piece tagged with where it came from.
02Match and draftEach objective is matched to the slides that cover it. Gaps are named, not filled with invented material. Drafts follow your sequence and your examples.
03CheckBullet limits, unexplained concepts, disguise leaks, facts that contradict the record, and citations no database has, all caught before you read the draft.
04 · youApprove and exportYou approve slide by slide. Final PowerPoint and Word files open only when everything is approved, and each carries its source record.
